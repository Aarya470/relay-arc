# Relay Arc

Free emergency web app for families who live far from a hospital, built first for people with heart conditions and dysautonomia in central New Jersey.

## What it does
- SOS. Say or type what is happening. The phone works out the kind of emergency, even with no signal.
- Picks a hospital that can treat it, by real drive time and by what each hospital can do (New Jersey Department of Health data for heart attacks). If help is 25 minutes or more away it says to call 911 first.
- Directions inside the app.
- Texts the family the hospital and a live location link, and sends medical details ahead.
- Emergency card with no account. Blood type, allergies, medicines, who to call. Kept on the phone, works offline.
- Pulse from the phone camera, breathing from the motion sensor, a NEWS2 score a clinician can read.
- Short daily check in, medicines, symptoms.

## Who uses it
Patients and their families, often older, often stressed, often on a cheap phone with weak signal. Read in a hurry. Large text, plain words, one clear action.

## Voice
Plain, calm, everyday words. Short sentences. No hyphens or colons in page copy. No "not this, but that" lines. One heading per section. Never claims to diagnose. Always says to call 911 in an emergency.

## Facts that must stay true
Free. Nonprofit. No ads, no trackers. Partners are BBSCA, Aerox Labs (reviewed the models) and East Windsor Board of Education (in discussion, not a formal partner).
